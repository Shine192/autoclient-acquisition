# Honest, helpful freelance outreach

The one-review workflow stays: AI research → review the main/follow-up pair in
Notion → STATUS = Approve → guarded delivery.

Prioritise Klaviyo/email/retention agencies serving e-commerce. Published services
or projects prove relevance, not hiring, budget, intent or a capacity problem.
Use relevant published contact routes. Posted applications and authorised HR
work enquiries remain distinct from business introductions.

Both AI handoff and automatic research share this writing structure: verified
observation → relevant Figma/Klaviyo contribution → portfolio → selected offered
price → one low-pressure reply invitation. Aim for 90–130 words before signature, in short
paragraphs. No generic praise, invented results, unsupported diagnoses, fake
urgency, or fabricated prior conversation. Initial cold subjects cannot use a
misleading Re:/Fwd: prefix.

Offer one small paid Figma test from client-supplied copy/assets, with desktop
and mobile layouts. Scope and timing are agreed before starting. Klaviyo building
is separately quoted. Do not promise availability or unverified performance.

Introduce the website with “You can review my recent work and learn more about
my services here: [View my portfolio](the selected portfolio URL).”
Close with “If this fits your needs, feel free to reply and we can discuss a
small paid test email, with no commitment to ongoing work.” Use this as the
single invitation in both the main email and follow-up. Selected quotes remain separate
from client budget. No extra review step is added. The single no-reply follow-up
after two business days adds practical scope, instead of repeating the opener.
Any reply stops it; actual responses remain review-only.

Only unapproved, uncontacted cold drafts are refreshed. Source observations are
verified against saved documents. If a suitable short observation is missing,
preserve the original main draft. Sent/approved messages, the authorised HR
enquiry, terms, receipts, evidence and suppression/history are preserved.

Measure genuine positive replies, paid tests, repeat clients and acquisition
minutes against explicit denominator counts. Compare audiences and offered
quotes; test one variable at a time and treat small samples as directional.

463 local tests pass, including eight copy-strategy regressions. No outreach or
approval was performed. The owner approved this strategy release;
Notion draft revisions are already applied with originals backed up.
