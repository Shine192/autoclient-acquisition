# Ten-minute agency acquisition workflow

Focus: small agencies offering Klaviyo/email marketing to e-commerce clients.
The first experiment is 40 qualified agencies over four weeks: 10 each in
Australia, the UK, Dubai and EU countries. Agency size 2–50 is a preference,
not an exclusion. EU is a region; choose an actual country/city for research.
Other markets, company types and existing records remain available.

## Each working day

1. Two minutes: review replies, stops and delivery holds.
2. Five minutes: review approximately two new suitable prospects in Notion.
3. Three minutes: review follow-up drafts and record client results/time.

Use the Mac dashboard's **Client development → Refresh from Notion**. The new
Notion views are **5 · Agency development** and **6 · Reviewed follow-ups**.
The experiment starts when the first qualified agency is enrolled; the dashboard
then shows the four-week review date. It does not create a dated sending campaign,
start research, spend API credit, approve prospects or send mail.

## Pricing and portfolio

For a new prospect select USD 25–30/email (default) or USD 30–35/email manually.
**Design Quote** is your offered price; **Rate Per Email**, **Compensation** and
**Budget Currency** remain published client compensation. Drafts and final sender
checks use the selected quote. A mismatch is held; the system does not rewrite it.
Previously approved/sent quotes stay intact. Put an unsent record into Needs Review
before changing its quote or message. The existing IR and Prism quote decisions
remain valid when their new quote field is blank.

New drafts use the curated portfolio through the prospect's **Portfolio Link**.
Existing approved messages retain their original link and exact wording. The hosted
sender accepts the owner-confirmed curated link without replacing its legacy global
profile or resetting policy approval. Portfolio client names stay out of email text.

Paid test: one Figma email, desktop and mobile layouts, supplied copy/assets and
one revision round. Klaviyo implementation is quoted separately. Turnaround is
agreed individually. No revenue or conversion results are claimed.

## Reviewed follow-ups — automatic delivery disabled

Prepare and edit up to two drafts in Client development. Each has its own review
decision and delivery state. Review does not create an outbox item or send mail.

The first review date is five business days after the saved initial send. The
second is seven business days after the first follow-up's actual send. Until the
previous receipt exists, the next date remains unset. Weekends are skipped;
public holidays are not inferred. Dates are review dates, not dispatch bookings.

Review requires the original saved receipt and conversation thread. A changed
recipient, source, initial message, quote, portfolio, timezone or follow-up draft
invalidates its saved review. Replies, rejection, opt-out, bounce, suppression or
uncertain delivery hold the process. The hosted sender still rejects automatic
follow-ups. Activation needs a separate request and sender implementation review.

If you send a reviewed follow-up yourself in Gmail, reply in its existing thread.
Use **Record existing send** to log its actual receipt and timestamp. This is a
manual report, not verification by Gmail, and the app does not send that message.
Keep every receipt and initial-contact reservation; do not reset initial STATUS
to Approve to make a follow-up.

## Client results

Record each outcome in order: positive reply → paid test agreed → paid test
completed → repeat client. Mark automated replies separately. Add actual outcome
dates and research/review/conversation minutes; production time is separate.
Saves retain a dated history and repeated requests do not add minutes twice.

- Positive reply rate: unique manually confirmed positive replies / initial recipients with saved receipts.
- Paid-test agreement rate: confirmed agreements / initial recipients with saved receipts.
- Completion rate: completed paid tests / agreed paid tests.
- Repeat-client rate: repeat clients / completed paid tests.

Compare counts by region and offered quote; an empty denominator stays unknown.
Review the first four weeks as directional evidence, not a statistically proven winner.

Projects: Happy Dog, Retronanix and Tapijtenloods. Shine confirmed Figma design
and Klaviyo implementation for all three; copy was supplied. Names are authorised
in the portfolio, not outreach email wording. Original project files are preserved.

A website can follow the same structure: introduction → three selected projects →
services and paid-test scope → process/contact. Build it as a responsive one-page
site first, with a larger project view for each example and a clear enquiry link.
Use the approved work and contribution text; keep results unclaimed unless verified.
Choose the domain/hosting and approve public publication separately. No website
was published or domain purchased as part of this update.
