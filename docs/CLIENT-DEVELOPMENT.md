# AI → Notion review → Approve → automatic delivery

## Your four steps

1. Search with AI in the Mac dashboard, or research with your preferred AI and
   import its sourced results into the existing app. Research still runs on your
   Mac; delivery runs on GitHub. Import validates the source and prepares Notion.
2. Open the Notion record. Review the recipient/contact purpose, main email,
   offered price and reason, and ONE no-reply follow-up. A reply template is also
   visible. The price and follow-up are prepared automatically; no enrolment or
   follow-up-approval visit is required for a new complete sequence.
3. Set the actual **STATUS = Approve** once. This authorizes exactly the visible
   main email and one no-reply follow-up. It never authorizes reply negotiation.
4. GitHub sends the main email within company-local weekday 08:00–11:00 and
   sends the single follow-up after two company-local business days if no reply.
   Weekends are skipped; public holidays are not inferred. Shared daily limits,
   spacing, source/contact checks, suppression and duplicate protection remain.

Any reply, opt-out, rejection, bounce, manual-send conflict or delivery
uncertainty stops follow-ups. An inbound reply gets a scope-clarification draft
for your review; it is never automatically answered. Review the actual message
before editing/sending a reply. No second no-reply follow-up can be delivered.

## Price selection

Published team size of 50 or fewer → USD 25–30 per designed email. A sourced
lower bound above 50 → USD 30–35. Unknown size → USD 25–30, explicitly marked
unknown. This is an offered quote, not a confirmed client budget. No employee
count is fabricated. You can override **Design Quote** and revise the main
message before approval. Klaviyo building remains separately quoted. Posted
hourly compensation stays separate and is never converted to a per-email rate.

## Approval and changes

Initial queueing saves an immutable fingerprint of the selected recipient,
main message, follow-up, source evidence, portfolio link, quote and timezone.
Editing those details holds delivery for renewed review. To withdraw an unsent
approval use Needs Review; to stop the remainder of a sent sequence use
**Sequence Mode = Hold**, or Do Not Contact where appropriate. Initial sent
messages and provider receipts are never replaced. Existing sent records do not
silently acquire sequence authorization. Their legacy approval/history remains.

Portfolio wording is **View my portfolio**, linked to
https://shine-email-studio.htooaungshine789.chatgpt.site . Email transport includes
both a clickable HTML label and a plain-text URL fallback; arbitrary message
content is escaped. No prior client names or unverified results are claimed.

## Results without an enrolment step

Complete qualified sequence records and sent sequences enter the results cohort
automatically; legacy enrolled experiment records remain included. Record actual
positive replies, paid tests agreed/completed, repeat clients and acquisition
minutes. Automated responses are separate. Do not invent work outcomes.

After four weeks compare positive replies / unique initial sends, agreed paid
tests / initial sends, completed tests / agreed tests, repeat clients / completed
tests, and minutes per agreed test. Compare counts by market and selected quote;
empty denominators are unknown and small samples remain directional.

## Release and existing history

This release implements the one-approval workflow for prepared sequence records.
Existing sent records retain their original authorization and history; they do not
silently gain a sequence approval. Restart the updated Mac dashboard before new
AI research. Local sending remains draft-only while GitHub owns delivery.
